# Aptitude Preparation Module - Quick Start Guide 🚀

## 📍 Access the Module

**Category List**: http://localhost:5174/aptitude  
**Practice Page**: http://localhost:5174/aptitude/{category-name}

---

## 🎯 Quick Start

### **Step 1: Login**
- Visit: http://localhost:5174/login
- Login to your account (required for progress tracking)

### **Step 2: Browse Categories**
- Go to: http://localhost:5174/aptitude
- See 6 category cards with statistics

### **Step 3: Choose a Category**
Click "Start Practice" on any category:
- ⏰ **Time & Work** - Work rates and efficiency
- 📈 **Time & Distance** - Speed and motion
- **%** **Percentages** - Percentage calculations
- 💰 **Profit & Loss** - Business math
- 🎲 **Probability** - Chance and combinations
- 🧩 **Logical Reasoning** - Patterns and series

### **Step 4: Practice Questions**
- Read the question carefully
- Select one of 4 options
- Click "Submit Answer"
- View detailed solution
- Learn from explanations

### **Step 5: Track Progress**
- Watch your accuracy percentage
- See score in real-time
- Review completed questions

### **Step 6: Improve Weak Areas**
- Click "Retry Wrong Questions"
- Practice only incorrect answers
- Improve your accuracy

- Begin with Easy questions

- Understand the approach
- Learn the formulas

### **3. Watch Videos**
- Click YouTube links for visual learning
- See problems solved in real-time
- Get additional tips

### **4. Track Accuracy**
- Aim for 70%+ accuracy
- Retry wrong questions
- Focus on weak categories

### **5. Practice Daily**
- Spend 15-20 minutes daily
- Cover different categories
- Improve speed with timer

## 📱 Navigation

### **From Dashboard**
1. Go to Dashboard
2. Click "Aptitude Preparation" card
3. Lands on category list

### **From Category List**
1. Click "Start Practice" on any category
2. Opens practice page with first question
3. Practice all questions in that category

### **During Practice**
- **← Back**: Return to category list
- **Previous**: Go to previous question
- **Next**: Go to next question
- **Retry Wrong**: Practice incorrect answers

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Solution**: Check if you're logged in (required for tracking)

### **Issue**: No statistics showing
**Solution**: Statistics appear after first attempt
